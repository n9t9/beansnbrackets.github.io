Lumen Guard

Comfortable viewing, on your terms.

Fine-tune your Android display with extra dimming, adjustable warmth, saved scenes, and schedules that fit your routine.

Get it on Google Play See Support

Lumen Guard adjusts your display appearance; it is not a medical device or a substitute for professional eye care.

Powerful Features

Adjustable Screen Dimming

Use the overlay to make a bright display more comfortable in low-light environments.

Warmth Control

Choose the colour warmth that suits what you are doing, from neutral to a softer evening tint.

Custom Scenes

Save your preferred dim and warmth combinations, then return to them without rebuilding your settings.

Smart Scheduling

Set a start and end time for a scene so your preferred display setup can follow your daily routine.

Floating Controls

Keep quick filter controls close at hand with the optional floating widget when it is enabled.

Respectful Notifications

Notifications are optional. The filter still works if you leave them off; you simply will not receive status updates or reminders.

Designed Around Android

Lumen Guard uses Android's display-overlay permission only to show the filter across your screen.

Privacy Choices

Core settings stay on your device. The free version includes advertising, with privacy choices shown in the app where required.
