Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 16, 2026

Our Privacy Commitment

Your privacy matters to us. Lumen Guard stores its display settings locally. Its free version uses Google Mobile Ads, which processes certain information to provide, measure, and protect advertising. This policy explains both practices.

This Privacy Policy explains how Lumen Guard ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). By using Lumen Guard, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information Collection Overview

Lumen Guard has two separate data practices:

  • Core app settings: filter preferences, scenes, schedules, and related local settings are kept on the device.
  • Advertising: the free version uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK for banner ads and optional rewarded ads. Google processes data as described in the advertising section below.
  • Premium: premium removes the app's advertising placements. Google Play still processes purchase information under its own privacy policy.

Information We (Lumen Guard) DO NOT Collect

Lumen Guard does not ask you to create an account or directly collect the following through its core display controls:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
  • Names, email addresses, or phone numbers
  • Precise location data
  • Screen content or screenshots
  • Browsing history or the content of other apps

Important: Google's Mobile Ads SDK has its own data practices. The SDK data is described below and must be considered separately from the locally stored app settings.

Data Storage

App settings and preferences are stored locally on your device. This data includes:

  • Dim and warmth level preferences
  • Custom scene configurations
  • Schedule settings
  • App configuration options, including notification and widget preferences

These local settings are removed if you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. This does not control data processed independently by Google or Google Play.

Permissions We Require

Lumen Guard requires the following Android permissions to function:

1. SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW (Draw Over Other Apps)

This permission is essential for Lumen Guard to display the dimming overlay on top of other applications. This is the core functionality that allows the app to dim your screen and apply the blue light filter system-wide.

2. FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE

These permissions allow Lumen Guard to keep the active overlay available while you switch to other apps. They are used for the filter service, not to read screen content.

3. Notifications, boot recovery, Internet, and Google Play Billing

Notifications are optional and are used for filter status and optional reminders. Boot recovery lets enabled schedules be restored after a restart. Internet access supports Google Mobile Ads and Google Play billing. Google Play Billing is used only when you choose to make a premium purchase.

Important: “Display over other apps” is required for the filter to work. Notification access is optional. The app does not use overlay access to capture screen content.

Advertising and Third-Party Services

Third-Party Advertising (Free Version Only)

The free version of Lumen Guard displays banner ads and offers optional rewarded ads through Google Mobile Ads. Google may process information to provide advertising, measure performance, and prevent fraud.

Information Collected by Advertising Networks

The current Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares the following categories for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention:

  • IP address: which may be used to estimate the device's general location.
  • Product interactions: including app launches, taps, ad views, and other ad interactions.
  • Diagnostic information: such as app and SDK performance information.
  • Device and account identifiers: including the Android advertising ID, app set ID, and applicable signed-in account identifiers.

Purpose of Data Collection

Google uses this information to:

  • Deliver relevant advertisements to you
  • Measure ad performance and engagement
  • Detect and prevent fraud
  • Improve advertising services
  • Comply with legal obligations

Google Mobile Ads

Lumen Guard currently uses Google Mobile Ads. Google explains its data handling and advertising practices in the resources below:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure

Your Advertising Choices

Where required, Lumen Guard presents Google's consent form. If Google requires a persistent privacy-options entry point, it appears in the app under Settings → Privacy choices, where you can update your advertising choices. You can also use Android and Google controls for your advertising ID.

How to Opt-Out of Personalized Ads on Android:

  1. Open your device Settings
  2. Go to Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads)
  3. Review the available ad-privacy controls
  4. Reset or delete your advertising ID if that option is available on your device

Note: Opting out means you'll still see ads, but they won't be personalized based on your interests.

Additional opt-out options:

  • Digital Advertising Alliance Opt-Out
  • European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance

Remove Ads Entirely - Upgrade to Premium

Premium removes Lumen Guard's in-app advertising placements. It does not change Google Play's separate handling of purchase information. Premium users receive:

  • Zero ads - No advertisements whatsoever
  • Plus all premium features (unlimited scenes, advanced scheduling, etc.)

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We want to be completely transparent about when and how data is shared:

Data Shared by Lumen Guard

We do not operate a user account, profile, or separate analytics service for Lumen Guard's core controls. Google receives SDK data when ads are requested or displayed, as described in its privacy documentation.

Data Shared by Advertising Networks

Google may share data with:

  • Advertising Partners: Ad networks that bid to show you advertisements
  • Analytics Providers: To measure ad performance and campaign effectiveness
  • Fraud Prevention Services: To detect and prevent invalid ad traffic
  • Service Providers: Companies that help operate the advertising platform

Circumstances for Data Disclosure

Data may be disclosed in the following limited circumstances:

  • Legal Compliance: When required by law, court order, or government request
  • Safety and Security: To protect the rights, property, or safety of users or the public
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale (users will be notified)
  • With Consent: With your explicit consent for specific purposes

Internet Permission

Lumen Guard requires the INTERNET permission for Google Mobile Ads in the free version and Google Play billing. This permission allows Google services to:

  • Download and display ad content
  • Communicate with advertising servers
  • Report ad performance metrics

Premium version note: Premium removes the app's advertising placements. Google Play billing continues to be governed by Google's own terms and privacy policy.

In-App Purchases

Lumen Guard offers an optional premium upgrade through Google Play's in-app purchase system. When you make a purchase:

  • The transaction is processed entirely by Google Play
  • We do not receive or store your payment information
  • Google Play's privacy policy governs the collection and use of payment data
  • We only receive a confirmation that the purchase was successful

Please review Google's Privacy Policy for information on how they handle payment data.

Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Lumen Guard is not designed for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information through the app's core controls from children under 13.

  • Advertising data is handled by Google under its own privacy policy and applicable controls.
  • If you believe a child has provided personal information to us directly, contact us so we can review the request.

Parents and guardians can contact us at beansnbrackets@gmail.com.

Data Security

We take data security seriously:

  • Local settings: display preferences are kept in the app's local storage.
  • Advertising data: Google states that data collected by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS.
  • Third-party security: Google maintains its own security practices. Review Google's privacy documentation for details.

No internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security. Please review Google's privacy policy for advertising and billing data.
